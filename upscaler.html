Good to know

  • The AI model (Real-ESRGAN, MIT licensed) runs fully on your device. Photos are never uploaded.
  • It makes small photos bigger and cleaner-looking by inventing plausible detail. It cannot recover true detail that was lost from the original.
  • First use downloads the AI model and its runtime (a few MB). Your browser caches them, so later uses are faster.
  • Fastest in Chrome or Edge (uses your GPU when available, CPU otherwise). Large photos take longer.
